This website uses a small number of cookies. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Essential cookies remember the choice you make about cookies; personalisation cookies are only set if you agree to them.

## What Cookies We Use

We use only essential and personalisation cookies:

- **Essential cookies**: These are used to remember your cookie choice.
- **Personalisation cookies**: These are used to personalise your experience using Optimizely Web Experimentation. They are only set if you choose **Accept all**.

We do **not** use cookies for advertising, tracking across other websites, or analytics.

## Managing Cookies

You can change your choice at any time using **Cookie settings** in the footer of every page. If you withdraw your consent, the personalisation cookies are removed.

You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies entirely or on a site-by-site basis.

## Cookies

The following cookies are in use:

- accept_cookies
  - **Type:** Essential
  - **Usage:** Used to remember whether you have made a cookie choice.
  - **Duration:** 365 Days
- accept_personalization
  - **Type:** Essential
  - **Usage:** Used to remember whether you have opted in to personalisation cookies.
  - **Duration:** 365 Days
- optimizelyEndUserId
  - **Type:** Personalisation
  - **Usage:** Used by Optimizely Web Experimentation to ensure consistent use of variations for a website visitor.
  - **Duration:** 180 Days
- optimizelySession
  - **Type:** Personalisation
  - **Usage:** Used by Optimizely Web Experimentation to track the start of a single browsing session. Typically contains a date as a number.
  - **Duration:** 180 Days

## Local Storage

If you use the light and dark mode switch, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the name `theme` so that it is remembered on your next visit. This is not a cookie, it is never sent to this website's server, and it can be cleared through your browser settings.
